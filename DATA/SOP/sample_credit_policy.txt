Credit Policy – Personal Loan Underwriting Guidelines (Excerpt)

Eligibility
Applicants must be resident individuals aged 21–60 years with a minimum net monthly income of INR 25,000 and a credit bureau score of 700 or above unless approved under exception policy.

Documentation
Mandatory documents include identity proof, address proof, income proof (salary slips or ITR), and bank statements for the last six months.

Debt Service Coverage
Total EMI obligations including the proposed loan must not exceed 50% of net monthly income unless compensating factors are documented and approved by credit committee.

Loan Amount
Maximum personal loan amount is capped at 24 times net monthly income or INR 25,00,000, whichever is lower, subject to product-specific limits.

Approval Matrix
Loans up to INR 5,00,000 may be approved by branch credit officer. Amounts above INR 5,00,000 require regional credit head approval. Exceptions require committee review.

Prohibited Use
Loan proceeds must not be used for speculative trading, cryptocurrency investment, or any activity prohibited under internal AML policy.
